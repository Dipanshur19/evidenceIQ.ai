## 🖥️ SLIDE 1 (Core 1): The Problem — The 3 Fatal Breakdowns in Modern Enterprise BI

### Slide Title
**Why Traditional Dashboards and Naive LLM Agents Fail in High-Stakes Operations**

---

### Layout & Visual Elements
* **3 Threat Cards with Icons (🔴):**
  1. **Alert Fatigue & Fragmented Data Silos**
  2. **The "Hallucination Trap" in Quantitative Analytics**
  3. **Ungoverned / Unsafe Autonomous Execution**

---

### Slide Content (Bullet Points)
* **Breakdown 1: Siloed, Out-of-Context Alerts:**
  * ERP alerts notify teams that revenue dropped by 68%, but deployment logs and customer support ticket streams live in completely separate databases. Root cause triage takes hours of manual correlation.
* **Breakdown 2: Naive LLM Arithmetic & Speculative Hallucinations:**
  * Generic LLM agents suffer from arithmetic errors when calculating $z$-scores, baseline deviations, and PVM drivers, fabricating plausible-sounding but mathematically false causes.
* **Breakdown 3: The Danger of Ungoverned Autonomous Actions:**
  * Autonomous agents triggering unreviewed rollbacks or budget changes introduce severe operational risk without rollback plans or auditability.

---

### Speaker Notes (35s)

Today, teams spend 4+ hours digging through logs across separate silos. Naive LLMs cannot solve this because they hallucinate math. We built EvidenceIQ.ai to solve these three critical enterprise breakdowns."*

## 🖥️ SLIDE 2 (Core 2): The Solution — Decoupled Graph-First Intelligence Architecture

### Slide Title
**A Deterministic Foundation Coupled with Language Synthesis & Human Governance**

---

### Layout & Visual Elements
* **Architectural Flowchart (5 Connected Blocks):**
  `1. Multi-Source Ingestion` ➔ `2. Deterministic Anomaly & PVM` ➔ `3. Business Evidence Graph` ➔ `4. Guardrailed LLM Narration` ➔ `5. Human Checkpoint & Action`
* **Key Architecture Principle Callout Box:** *"Strict Separation of Quantitative Math (0ms LLM) from Natural Language Synthesis."*

---

### Slide Content (Bullet Points)
* **Core Philosophy: Math in Code, Narrative in LLM:**
  * Statistical calculations, $z$-score bounds, and PVM waterfall math execute entirely in deterministic Python/Node.js algorithms. LLMs are never asked to perform arithmetic.
* **Evidence-Graph Root Cause Isolation:**
  * Constructs dynamic relational graphs (`PRECEDES` and `CORROBORATES` edges) to rank explanatory drivers with mathematical confidence scores ($0.000 - 1.000$).
* **Privacy-First Local Execution:**
  * Fully operational on local **Ollama (`qwen2.5:1.5b`)**, ensuring $0.00 cloud API costs, sub-2-second inference, and zero enterprise data leakage.

---

### Speaker Notes (35s)
> *"Our core breakthrough is the strict decoupling of quantitative computation from language synthesis. Statistical baselines and Price-Volume-Mix decomposition run in deterministic code in milliseconds. The LLM only receives verified graph evidence, guaranteeing zero mathematical hallucinations."*

## 🖥️ SLIDE 7 (Core 7): Safe Abstention Under Uncertainty & Sparse History Governance

### Slide Title
**Communicating Uncertainty, Preventing Hallucination, and Handling Sparse KPIs**

---

### Layout & Visual Elements
* **Confidence Rating Tier Matrix:**
  * `HIGH (Score ≥ 0.75)` ➔ Auto-suggest primary action
  * `MEDIUM (0.45 ≤ Score < 0.75)` ➔ Multi-driver hypothesis inspection
  * `LOW / ABSTAIN (Score < 0.45)` ➔ Hard abstention & clarification request
* **Sparse History Card (`Central_India` / `Store_999`):** Showing 3 days history vs 14 required ➔ `is_sparse_history = True`.

---

### Slide Content (Bullet Points)
* **Hard Abstention on Insufficient Data:**
  * When queried on normal baseline dates (e.g. `2026-06-02`) or conflicting multi-driver signals, the engine returns status `"insufficient_data"` with confidence $< 0.450$ and explicitly refuses to guess.
* **Sparse-History / Newly Launched Metrics Protocol:**
  * Newly launched stores or regions with $<14$ days of history are automatically tagged with `is_sparse_history = True`.
  * Standard Gaussian $z$-score alerts are suppressed to prevent false positive noise, prompting analysts for baseline stabilization.
* **Fail-Closed Enterprise Safety:**
  * If upstream data feeds fail or schema checks fail validation, the engine safely halts processing rather than emitting degraded insights.

---

### Speaker Notes (30s)
> *"In enterprise decision-making, knowing when NOT to make a claim is as vital as the analysis itself. If data is insufficient or historical depth is under 14 days, EvidenceIQ.ai explicitly abstains, sets a LOW confidence tier, and requests additional data, preventing costly misinformed actions."*
